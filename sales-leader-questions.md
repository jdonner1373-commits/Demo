# Top Questions Sales Leaders Ask
## EMEA Sales Command Center - Executive Query Library

---

## Overview

This document contains 100+ realistic questions that Lee (EMEA Sales VP) and other sales leaders would ask the agent. These are organized by:
- **Use case** (what they're trying to accomplish)
- **Frequency** (how often they ask this)
- **Priority** (how valuable the answer is)
- **Demo potential** (how impressive it is to show)

---

## Category 1: Pipeline & Forecast Intelligence
*"What's in our pipeline and are we going to hit our number?"*

### Critical (Asked Multiple Times Per Week)

1. **"What's our total pipeline for this quarter?"**
   - Shows: $X.XM across Y opportunities
   - Breakdown by forecast category
   - Coverage vs target (need 3-4x)

2. **"What's our Commit forecast right now?"**
   - All deals in Commit forecast category
   - Total ARR, count of deals
   - Breakdown by segment

3. **"Are we on track to hit our $5.6M quarterly target?"**
   - Current position: $X.XM closed + $X.XM Commit
   - Gap to target: $X.XM
   - Probability assessment based on historical close rates

4. **"What changed in our forecast this week?"**
   - New deals added to Commit
   - Deals that moved up (Pipeline → Best Case → Commit)
   - Deals that slipped out or moved down
   - Net change in forecast

5. **"Show me our pipeline coverage by segment"**
   - Velocity: $X.XM pipeline, $X.XM target (X.Xx coverage)
   - Emerging Enterprise: [same format]
   - Major Enterprise: [same format]
   - Overall: X.Xx coverage (healthy = 3-4x)

6. **"What's our pipeline for next quarter?"**
   - Q1 pipeline looking ahead
   - Early indicators of health
   - Comparison to typical Q1 pipeline at this point

7. **"How does our pipeline compare to last quarter at this point?"**
   - Week-over-week comparison
   - Are we ahead or behind?
   - By how much?

8. **"What deals are stuck in negotiation?"**
   - Deals in negotiation stage > 30 days
   - Blockers or next steps
   - Owner and close date

### High Value (Asked Weekly)

9. **"What's our weekly pipeline generation?"**
   - New pipeline created this week
   - By segment, by AE
   - Trending up or down?

10. **"Show me pipeline by country"**
    - UK: $X.XM
    - Germany: $X.XM
    - France: $X.XM
    - Netherlands: $X.XM
    - Other: $X.XM

11. **"What's our conversion rate by stage?"**
    - Pipeline → Best Case: X%
    - Best Case → Commit: X%
    - Commit → Closed Won: X%
    - Overall Pipeline → Closed Won: X%

12. **"How accurate has our Commit forecast been?"**
    - Historical accuracy over last 3 quarters
    - % of Commit deals that actually closed
    - Identification of chronic over-forecasters

13. **"What's our average deal size by segment?"**
    - Velocity: $XXK
    - Emerging Enterprise: $XXK
    - Major Enterprise: $XXK
    - Trending larger or smaller?

14. **"Show me our sales velocity"**
    - Average time from Stage 1 → Closed Won
    - By segment, by region
    - Trending faster or slower?

15. **"What's our pipeline distribution by close date?"**
    - This month: $X.XM
    - Next month: $X.XM
    - Rest of quarter: $X.XM
    - Beyond quarter: $X.XM (should be small)

## Category 2: Deal Intelligence
*"What are our biggest deals and what's their status?"*

### Critical (Asked Daily)

16. **"What are our top 10 deals in EMEA right now?"**
    - Sorted by ARR
    - Account, ARR, stage, forecast, close date, owner
    - Links to Salesforce

17. **"What deals are closing this week?"**
    - All deals with close date in next 7 days
    - Stage and forecast category
    - Total ARR if all close

18. **"What deals closed this week?"**
    - All Closed Won from past 7 days
    - Total ARR closed
    - Celebration list for team

19. **"Show me all deals over $100K"**
    - Filtered by ARR threshold
    - Stage and forecast
    - Priority accounts

20. **"What are our biggest new business opportunities?"**
    - New Logo deals only
    - Sorted by ARR
    - Strategic accounts

21. **"What deals are in final stages right now?"**
    - Negotiation, Contract Sent, Verbal Commit stages
    - Imminent close opportunities

22. **"Show me multi-product deals"**
    - Deals with >1 product
    - Higher value, more complex
    - Need extra support

### High Value (Asked Weekly)

23. **"What deals did we create this week?"**
    - New opportunities created in last 7 days
    - Source (inbound, outbound, partner, etc.)
    - Total potential ARR

24. **"Show me all renewals closing next month"**

25. **"What are our biggest upsell opportunities?"**
    - Expansion deals with existing customers

    - Low-hanging fruit

26. **"What deals have been pushed more than once?"**
    - Close date changed 2+ times

27. **"Show me deals with custom pricing or discounts >20%"**

    - Competitive pressure indicator

28. **"What deals have executive involvement?"**
    - Dan or Tifenn in contacts/activities

29. **"Show me partner-sourced deals"**

30. **"What are our fastest-moving deals?"**

## Category 3: Risk & Health Analysis
*"What's at risk and what do we need to fix?"*

### Critical (Asked Multiple Times Per Week)

31. **"What Commit deals are at risk of slipping?"**
    - Deals in Commit forecast but showing risk signals
    - No activity in 7+ days
    - Long time in current stage
    - Close date approaching with low stage

32. **"What deals haven't been updated in 2 weeks?"**
    - Stale opportunities
    - Possible neglect
    - Need owner follow-up

33. **"Which renewals are at risk of churning?"**
    - Renewal opps with churn indicators
    - Low usage, support tickets, payment issues
    - Proactive save needed

34. **"What deals are going to slip from this quarter?"**
    - Close date in current quarter but low probability
    - Need to update forecast
    - Manage expectations

35. **"Show me deals with close dates in the past"**
    - Overdue opportunities
    - Need immediate attention
    - Data hygiene issue

36. **"What's our churn forecast for next quarter?"**
    - At-risk renewals by ARR
    - Proactive save opportunities
    - Retention rate projection

37. **"What deals have negative sentiment?"**
    - Based on recent Slack mentions, email tone, notes
    - Champion left, pricing objection, competitor threat
    - Need intervention

### High Value (Asked Weekly)

38. **"What's our win rate by segment this quarter?"**
    - Closed Won ÷ (Closed Won + Closed Lost)
    - By segment and overall
    - Trending better or worse?

39. **"What deals did we lose this month and why?"**
    - All Closed Lost
    - Loss reasons (competitor, no decision, budget, etc.)
    - Patterns to address

40. **"Show me deals where we're competing against [competitor]"**
    - Competitor field populated
    - Win rate vs this competitor
    - Competitive positioning

41. **"What's our average discount rate?"**
    - Across all deals
    - By segment
    - Margin impact

42. **"What deals have long sales cycles?"**
    - >120 days from Stage 1
    - Why are they taking so long?
    - Process improvement opportunity

43. **"Show me deals with missing information"**
    - No close date, no next step, no decision maker
    - Data quality issue
    - Need cleanup

44. **"What accounts have multiple open opportunities?"**
    - Complex accounts with several deals in flight
    - Coordination needed
    - Land-and-expand strategy

45. **"What's our pipeline that's actually realistic?"**
    - Remove stale deals (>6 months old, no activity)
    - Cleaned pipeline view
    - Honest assessment

## How to Use This List

### For Building the Agent:
- Start with Category 1-4 (core queries)
- Test 20-30 of these during development
- Use as examples in your system prompt
- Ensure Salesforce fields support these queries

### For Demo Day:
- Pick 5-7 from different categories
- Focus on Categories 2, 3, 7 (deal intelligence, risk, briefings)
- Include 1-2 from Category 12 ("wow factor")
- Practice until they work perfectly

### For Rolling Out:
- Share this list as "What You Can Ask"
- Create a "Top 25 Queries" quick reference
- Collect new questions from users
- Continuously expand capabilities

### For Pitch:
- Reference the breadth: "Can answer 100+ question types"
- Highlight frequency: "Lee asks 10-15 of these per week"
- Show categories: "From deals to performance to action generation"
- Emphasize time saved: "Each of these used to take 15-45 minutes"
