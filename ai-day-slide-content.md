## THE PROBLEM (Left box with blue icon)

**Title:** The Problem

**Content:**
Sales Operations spends 10-12 hours/week answering repetitive questions from leadership about deals, pipeline, and forecasts.

• EMEA Sales VP asks 15-20 questions weekly
• Each takes 15-45 minutes of manual work
• Pull Salesforce → Export to Sheets → Cross-reference trackers → Format → Send
• Leadership decisions delayed hours/days waiting for analysis
• Same questions repeated, no self-service option

"I spend half my week being a human query engine instead of a strategic partner."

## THE SOLUTION (Top right box with red beaker icon)

**Title:** The Solution

**Content:**
AI conversational agent that instantly answers sales questions using natural language

WHAT IT DOES:
• Queries Salesforce + Google Sheets + Slack in real-time
• Answers in 2 seconds vs 30 minutes
• Flags risks and opportunities proactively
• Generates executive briefings and messages

POWERED BY:
• Glean AI + custom 400-line system prompt
• 60+ query types across 13 categories
• EMEA sales domain expertise built-in

SAMPLE QUERIES:
"What are our top 10 EMEA deals?"
"Which Commit deals are at risk?"
"How is UK vs Germany performing?"
"Prep me for the CRO forecast call"
