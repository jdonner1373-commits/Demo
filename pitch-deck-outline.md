## Delivery Tips

**Slide Timing (10 min total):**
- Slides 1-3 (Problem & Solution): 2 min
- Slide 4-5 (How it works & teaser): 1 min
- Slides 6-7 (LIVE DEMO): 5 min ⭐ MOST IMPORTANT
- Slides 8-11 (Impact, roadmap, budget): 1.5 min
- Slide 15 (Call to action): 30 sec

**Backup slides (12-18):** Only if judges ask specific questions

**Design Guidelines:**
- Clean, minimal design (lots of white space)
- Amplitude brand colors
- Large, readable fonts (30pt minimum)
- One key idea per slide
- Visual > text where possible
- Use emojis sparingly for visual interest
- High-contrast (easy to read on projector)

**Rehearsal Checklist:**
- [ ] Practice full pitch 5+ times
- [ ] Time yourself (stay under 10 min)
- [ ] Test demo queries (make sure they work!)
- [ ] Get feedback from colleague
- [ ] Prepare for Q&A
- [ ] Print one-pagers for judges
- [ ] Have backup slides ready
- [ ] Charge laptop, test projector
- [ ] Breathe! You've got this 🚀
